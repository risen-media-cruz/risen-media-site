Common questions

What if our people are shy on camera?

That’s very common. I keep the setup simple, help everyone relax before we roll, and film real conversations instead of scripts, so their own voices come through.

Where do you film?

I come to you across Central Florida and bring the lighting and audio, so there’s nothing for your team to set up.

Do we have to sign up for something long-term?

Not for filmed projects. The Vision & Story and Equipping packages are one-time projects. The monthly Content Multiplier has a 3-month minimum and then continues month to month.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions. If you need more, extra rounds are $75 each. And if you’re still not happy after those two rounds, I’ll refund the editing portion of your project fee.

Can we keep the raw footage?

Yes. I can deliver all raw footage on a portable drive for +$250, so your media team can keep it for your archives.

Not sure which package fits?

That’s what the free 15-minute call is for. Tell me what you’re hoping to accomplish and I’ll recommend the best place to start.

Do you edit sermon clips too?

Yes. If you already record your services, see Sermon Clips. Your first 2 clips are free.

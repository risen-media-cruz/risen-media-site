Common questions

Which service is right for us?

If you already record your Sunday services, start with Sermon Clips. Your first 2 clips are free. If you want new video, like a testimonial or training series, filmed on location, see Video Production. Not sure? Book a free 15-minute call and I’ll point you in the right direction.

Do I have to choose just one?

No. You can start with sermon clips and add filmed video whenever you’re ready, or the other way around.

How do the 2 free clips work?

Send me one sermon and I’ll edit two clips for you at no charge. If you love them, we’ll set up your monthly plan. If not, you owe nothing.

Do I have to sign up for something long-term?

Sermon Clips has a 3-month minimum, then runs month to month, and you can cancel any time after that with 30 days’ notice. Filmed projects are one-time, except the monthly Content Multiplier, which also has a 3-month minimum.

Not sure where to start?

Book a free 15-minute call. Tell me what your church needs and I’ll point you to the right service. If you record your services, your first 2 clips are free.

I take on a limited number of churches each month, so every church gets my full attention.
